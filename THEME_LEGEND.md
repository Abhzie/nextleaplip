# Theme Legend

1. **Funds, KYC & Account Access** — KYC, login, verification, account access, withdrawals/redemptions and money movement.
2. **Orders & Trading** — order placement, execution, trading, stop-loss/target, F&O and positions.
3. **App Experience & Reliability** — crashes, lag, loading, speed, freezes, update-related performance.
4. **Support & Communications** — support tickets, responses, notifications, emails and communication.
5. **Product Requests** — feature requests, trackers, filters, dashboards, goal views and controls.
